# 3 Creek: concept brief

**Idea:** Drawn first, then laid. The hero is a 3D field of pavers that lands as an ink drawing and is then swept into real stone. Scrolling follows their actual four-step process: the camera rises to a plan view with dimension lines, colour washes in when materials are chosen, then it drops back to ground level for the build. A pattern lab lets a homeowner try five patterns in five colour blends.

**Why it suits them:** They say they provide a drawing with every project. That is their differentiator, and the current site mentions it in one line.

## What is on the page
1. Hero with the live paver field (it lifts under the cursor)
2. Process in four steps, driven by scroll
3. Pattern lab
4. Recent work by location
5. Materials: concrete, clay, stone and porcelain, permeable
6. What is underneath: base preparation and drainage, with a section drawing
7. Reviews, FAQ, contact

## Real, taken from their site or Google
- The four process steps, the four paver types, the base and drainage approach, the FAQ answers
- Blend names (Charleston Pearl, King St., River Run, Earthen Moon, Downtown Alley) are their own moodboard and product names
- Service areas, phone, email, the consultation request link
- Reviews and every photo

## Assumed, needs the client to confirm
- **Photo captions.** I matched photos to the locations described on their "Our Work" page by what is visible. Some may be wrong.
- The colours shown for each blend name are my interpretation.
- The dimension sheet (48 ft, sheet L-1, 2% slope) is decoration.
- The street address comes from their Google listing.
- "Ryan" is the name customers use in reviews.

## Tech
Static HTML, CSS and one JavaScript file. About 1,100 pavers drawn as one instanced mesh with a small custom shader in three.js. Layouts for each pattern are computed in code.
