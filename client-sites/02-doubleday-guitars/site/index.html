01/04 Body

One piece of wood. Not three glued together.

Each body is cut from a single lightweight plank of swamp ash or alder, chosen by hand for weight and ring before a saw touches it.

Wood
One-piece swamp ash or alder
Strings
Through-body or top loader
Hardware
Kluson, slot-head screws throughout

02/04 Neck

Carved to fit a hand, not a template.

Quarter-sawn maple, shaped with rasps and files until the profile is right for the person who ordered it. Tell Walker how you play and he'll tell you which carve.

Profile
Soft V or Chunky C
Radius
7.25", 9.5", 12" or 16"
Details
Bone nut, heel-adjust truss rod, Kluson Supreme 18:1

03/04 Pickups

Wound here, to the sound in your head.

Every Doubleday carries pickups scatter-wound by hand in the shop. You choose the magnet and how hot. The wiring is cloth pushback, the way it was done in the fifties.

Magnets
AlNiCo 2, 3, 5, 8 or ceramic
Wind
Low, classic or hot
Wiring
CTS pots, Mallory caps, shielded cavities

04/04 Finish

Thin nitro. It breathes, and it ages with you.

All-nitrocellulose lacquer, sprayed thin and buffed to a mirror. It lets the wood move and resonate, and it wears in honestly over the years you play it.

Lacquer
100% nitrocellulose, gloss or satin
Colour
Butterscotch
Guard
Bakelite, parchment or tortoise

The maker

Walker Doubleday answers his own email.

Walker graduated from the Roberto-Venn School of Luthiery in 2014, where he learned to build acoustic and electric guitars and took a particular interest in scatter-winding pickups by hand. He has been building in Austin ever since.

The shop runs on one idea: a guitar should feel personal. Not mass-produced, not rushed, and definitely not generic. When you order one, the person you talk to is the person holding the chisel.

“He put his work up against a Plek machine and matched it one to one. Awesome craftsmanship. Winds his own pickups.”Chip Waldroup, Google review

Ordering

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Talk it through

    Email Walker. He sends an order form and walks you through neck, wood, pickups and finish.

  2. 02

    Hold your place

    You get a written estimate. A 50% deposit puts you in the build queue.

  3. 03

    The build

    Current build time is under six months. You get updates from the bench along the way.

  4. 04

    It ships, set up

    Final setup, final payment, insured shipping. Covered by a five-year limited warranty.
